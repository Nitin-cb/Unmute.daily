Our Story

Why We Started UNMUTE

Two teachers. Hundreds of students. One recurring problem.

We met as co-workers teaching languages through screens. Somewhere between classes, student stories, and late-night conversations — a friendship began.

"Many learners understood the language but hesitated to speak."

That observation became the beginning of UNMUTE. We met learners from ages 5 to 60. Some wanted confidence. Some wanted opportunities. Some wanted connection. But most of them carried the same fear — they hesitated to speak.

What started as conversations between two teachers evolved into a learning space built around confidence, connection, and communication.
